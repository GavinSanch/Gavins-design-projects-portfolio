# A9 – Pulleys

## **Objective**

-Based on Pulley Dimensions/Pulley Material, RPM, and Material of Belt determine Diameter/Circumference/Part# on McMaster for belt

-Based on forces use stress and material of shaft to determine minimum diameter of the shaft

-Based on Machineries Handbook, Pressure, Velocity, and PV Factor find a suitable material to make the bearings out of

-Based on all these three factors design a frame that can support the pulley system

## **Part 1: Belt Selection**

The pictures below show my calculations, FBD, and knowns/unknowns for determining the correct belt off of McMaster. So, some things I decided to assume before digging into the belt selection was; N=2000 RPM, 6lbf Static Tension, Steel for Pulley Material (coefficient of friction between polyurethane rubber equals 0.6), and center distance between pulleys to be 6 inches. So, once that was settled, I put down my knowns and unknowns and labeled out my FBD. From there I ran out the calculations using equation three from the slides and F1+F2=12lbf, to help me determine the higher force F1 and the lower force F2. With F1, I used FOS to obtain allowable stress which in turn allowed me to find the diameter and circumference needed to determine my belt from McMaster.
